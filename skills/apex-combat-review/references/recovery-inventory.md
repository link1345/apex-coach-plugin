# Recovery, Reload, and Inventory

Judge opportunity cost, exposure, and continuation value. Do not score the action label itself.

## Required observations

- current health and shield as separate pools;
- healing items or deployables actually visible or referenced;
- enemy pressure, line of sight, and approach cues;
- cover and cancelability;
- magazine and backup weapon readiness;
- ally state and whether immediate trade support is needed;
- attack window from enemy healing, revive, or reload;
- movement during inventory or loot interaction.

## Recovery worksheet

Record:

- `resourceTypes`: health and/or shield;
- `availability`: confirmed, conditional, unavailable, or unknown;
- `deployed`: confirmed, not required, or unknown;
- `reachable`: confirmed, not required, or unknown;
- `completionWindow`: sufficient, pressured, or unknown;
- evidence ids.

Do not infer D.O.C. deployment from a subtitle or prompt. Do not infer reachability from the ability's existence. Do not describe D.O.C. as restoring shields unless the exact reference value supports that claim.

If audio pressure is unknown, branch the recommendation:

- no close pressure: attempt the appropriate short recovery;
- close pressure: use a cancelable recovery position or hold the threatened entry;
- ally enters an immediate close trade: compare the value of brief support with the durability gained.

## Inventory

Opening inventory is not automatically a mistake. Distinguish:

- short management while sliding or moving behind cover;
- stopping in a doorway, crossing, or enemy swing timing;
- lost line of sight or reaction readiness;
- whether teammates were already fighting;
- what the inventory action prevented or enabled.

Do not criticize the number of openings without showing time cost, movement loss, exposure, or a missed combat window.

## Reload and weapon swap

Compare current magazine, backup weapon, cover, enemy timing, and ally pressure. Do not declare one weapon universally correct at a range when peek style, swap time, ammo, and cover change the choice.
